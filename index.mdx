## The two sites

The most common mistake will be writing the wrong thing for the wrong property. One decision on 4 September settles it, and it governs every page, post and email here.

|  | Personal site · xherman.com | Experience Hacking · xhacking.eu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sells | The proof. Why Xavier | The method. What the Blueprint is |
| Carries | History, track record, references, the sizing argument, the entry price | Mechanics, the delivery spec, deliverables |
| Never carries | Technical mechanics, partner badges, the AI Ops Engine | No equivalent restriction |
| Reader leaves with | Conviction that he is the right person | Understanding of what they are buying |

## The one rule above all others

**Specifics instead of adjectives.** Every claim carries a number, a name or a source.

This is not a style preference. Xavier's audience is business owners who check. His own instinct is to correct a claim the moment it overstates, and he did that on the 4 September call against his own interest. Copy that overstates does not sound like him and will not survive contact with him.

<Warning icon="shield-alert">
  Nothing goes on a page in quotation marks unless it exists verbatim in a recording or on a published page. An earlier draft ran a testimonial under a real client's name containing sentences that were in no source. It was caught and removed. That standard is why the proof blocks sat empty for a month, and why they are now full of text that came straight off his profile.
</Warning>

## Language

Xavier publishes in English and French. This kit is written in English for the Boost33 team, and every ready-to-use block carries an FR tab beside the EN original.

<Note icon="languages">
