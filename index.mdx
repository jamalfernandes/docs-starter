## The offer, in one paragraph

The **AI Blueprint** is a custom diagnostic. An owner suspects AI should be doing something for the business and has no way to judge what. Xavier goes through the business with the people who run it, tests the real options on the company's own material, and comes back with a ranked roadmap that says where the money comes back and where it does not.

It is not a packaged product. A discovery call defines the scope, then the fee is fixed against it.

## What changed on 21 September

Xavier returned seven pages of corrections on the 4 September mockup. Four of them were factual, and one was a live credibility risk.

| What was wrong | What it is now |
| --- | --- |
| Hero said he worked "inside" Audi, Nike, Nestlé, Canal+, Club Med | He built it for them from the agency side, then ran it from the inside elsewhere |
| Target size given as "two to ten" people | Independents and companies up to twenty to fifty, depending on sector |
| The Blueprint takes "one week" | Two to four weeks, depending on scope. Three is typical |
| "I've got no build to sell you after" | False. The AI Ops Engine is exactly that build |
