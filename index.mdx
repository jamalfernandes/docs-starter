## The two sites

The most common mistake will be writing the wrong thing for the wrong property. It is one decision, made on 4 September, and it governs everything.

|  | Personal site · xherman.com | Experience Hacking · xhacking.eu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sells** | The proof. Why Xavier. | The method. What the Blueprint is. |
| **Carries** | History, track record, references, the sizing argument | Mechanics, deliverables, price |
| **Never carries** | Price, technical mechanics, partner badges, AI Ops Engine | — |
| **Reader leaves with** | Conviction that he is the right person | Understanding of what they are buying |

## The one rule above all others

**Specifics instead of adjectives.** Every claim carries a number, a name or a source.

This is not a style preference. Xavier's audience is business owners who check, and his own instinct is to correct a claim the moment it overstates — he did exactly that on the 4 September call, against his own interest. Copy that overstates does not sound like him and will not survive contact with him.

<Warning>
  Nothing goes on a page in quotation marks unless it exists verbatim in a recording or something already published. An earlier draft carried a testimonial under a real client's name containing sentences that were not in any source. It was found and removed. That is the standard now, and it is why the proof blocks are blank rather than full.
</Warning>

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the block you need">
    Use the search bar. Every ready-to-use text sits in a copy block with a copy button, so nothing has to be retyped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the brackets">
    Anything in `[square brackets]` needs a real detail from Xavier. A post with an invented number does more damage than no post at all, because this audience checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it against the voice rules">
    One pass over [the do-and-don't list](/voice). If a draft cannot be traced back to a [pillar](/messaging), and that pillar to a proof point, it does not publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build from the token sheet">
    The [style sheet](/visual) is copy-ready CSS. It drops straight into the repo — no interpretation step between this kit and the build.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changed on 4 September

Two things, and both are settled. Do not reopen them in drafts.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The differentiator is honest sequencing, not refusal" icon="git-branch">
    An earlier draft argued that Xavier has **no build to sell afterwards**, and that this was the whole differentiator because every competitor has one.

    Xavier corrected it on the call: it is not true. There is a build, and he can provide it.

    The replacement keeps the spine and drops the false claim:

    > The Blueprint's job is to tell you where AI pays off — including when the answer is nowhere. What you do next is your call.

    He tells you the truth before anyone asks you to buy anything. If a build is the right answer, he says so. Never write, imply or resurrect _no build to sell_.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Blueprint is seven days, flat" icon="calendar">
    Stated as one week, every time, in every language. No _typically_, no _around_, no _depending on scope_.

    A concrete number manages expectations better than a hedge, even when the work spans two calendar weeks. The commitment is the time it takes from the owner, not the wall-clock span.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Language

Xavier publishes in **English and French**.

This kit is written in English as the working reference for the Boost33 team. Every ready-to-use copy block carries an **FR** tab alongside the **EN** original.

<Note>
  The French is written, not translated. Short sentences stay short, _vous_ throughout, no em dashes, and no anglicism Xavier would not say out loud. He knows how his own market talks, so he reads the FR before anything publishes.
</Note>
