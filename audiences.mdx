## The rule that governs this page

**Audiences are defined by the problem they carry, not by job title.** Xavier's instruction, not a preference. On 12 August:

> "the persona may change from one company to another. When in big groups, they usually have the same job. Under the same title, they have the same job."

An SME has the same functions as a large group, carried by fewer people: *"one person can have like several functions."* Sometimes the CEO is also the technical lead. A title-based persona breaks on contact. A problem-based one holds. Do not reintroduce job titles as segments anywhere in this kit.

---

## Primary audience: the SME owner with unanswered AI questions

**The problem they carry.** They believe they should be doing something with AI. They cannot say what. They fear spending and getting nothing back. Xavier, voicing the buyer on 12 August:

> "I don't understand what's happening on this market, I don't understand what I can do, and where am I going to put like every euro, where can I file return on investments before I put any euro into production of something, okay?"

**What they already tried.** Something, and it failed: _"we have... We many things, nothing worked. So we lost a lot of money."_ Treat prior failure as the default. And they expect AI to run itself: _"AI is not a magic wand."_

**What they are actually buying.** Not technology. A decision they can defend to themselves: where the money goes, and where it must not. The purchase has to be smaller than the fear. That is what seven days buys.

**Their vocabulary is not ours.** On _friction_: _"the term friction doesn't talk a lot to people, except those are already mature."_ On the Experience Hacking site: _"for most of the SMEs, it's still too complicated."_ Write in money, waste, where to start.

**Where they look.** Unresolved. Every deal to date came from Xavier's network, the problem this engagement exists to fix. [awaiting Xavier, which channels produced the Blueprint enquiries to date]

## The Comex / Codir motion: same motion, larger company

Not a second audience. Xavier, 12 August:

> "It's usually from the business owner slash CEO, which are usually my entry points. [...] involving all his management team, like executive, okay?"

The entry point is the same person as the primary audience. The executive team is who that person brings into the room afterwards. One motion with a second step, not a different buyer. Same door, bigger room. At 25 people the management team may be three, one of them the CEO.

Keep Comex/Codir as a description of how the work runs inside a company. It is never a large-corporate pitch: _"I don't want to work anymore with big companies."_

## Who this is not for

**The SME that already has an AI-capable person internally.** Decided on the 4 September call: say plainly that this is not for everyone. A company with someone already driving AI can allocate its own budget without a Blueprint. Saying so makes the rest of the page more believable and stops a bad-fit enquiry consuming a call.

**The routing.** They go to the Friction Scanner, the free lead magnet. It keeps a qualified company in contact without a purchase they do not need. *Friction* lands with this group because they have already been through it. Right destination here, wrong headline above.
