## One job per surface

Seven surfaces are live. Each one gets a single job. A surface doing two jobs does neither, which is the condition the July and September audits found across the estate: three properties describing the same diagnostic work in three vocabularies, and the credibility sitting on a different domain from the commerce.

| Surface | Its one job | Who it is for | What it must never do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **xherman.com** | Sell the proof. Establish that Xavier's judgement is worth buying. | Someone who has just heard his name and is checking him before a conversation. | Carry price, mechanics, package lists, partner badges, or the AI Ops Engine. |
| **Experience Hacking** · xhacking.eu | Sell the method. The Blueprint, its five steps, the buyer's time cost, the price. | A reader already convinced by the person and now asking what the thing actually is. | Be the first surface a stranger meets. Keep advertising past-dated webinars as live, which it currently does. |
| **LinkedIn** | Start the conversation the site finishes. | The owner or CEO inside his network and one degree out from it. | Speak in an agency voice. Replies in the thread are Xavier's, or they do not happen. |
| **Lex Index** | Nothing, here. A footer line proving he ships products. | Lawyers, who do not arrive through anything in this kit. | Appear above the footer, or take a single slot in the weekly cadence. |
| **Friction Scanner** | Hold a qualified company that should not buy a Blueprint yet. | The SME with someone internally already driving AI, and the curious reader not ready to talk. | Be dressed up as a small Blueprint, or be described with the word *friction* to a reader who has not been through it. |
| **Writing** · the three published articles | Give a reader who wants more than a post somewhere to go next. | The reader who clicked twice and is still reading. | Run on a cadence Xavier cannot keep. Stay bylined to the team, which hands the credibility to the brand instead of the person. |
| **Speaking** | Teach in the room. Sell nothing. | Event organisers, panel bookers, and the management team once a CEO opens the door. | Become a large-corporate pitch. That market is the one he is leaving. |

The AI Ops Engine is not a public surface anywhere in this kit. It is the delivery platform, used once a project is already decided. `[xh-consulting.com and xhacking.app: retire, redirect, or keep — awaiting Xavier]`

## LinkedIn is the engine

Not because LinkedIn is a good channel in the abstract. Because three things Xavier already owns sit there and sit nowhere else.

His profile is the **first result on his name**, ahead of his own site, confirmed on 28 July. Whatever a prospect concludes about him, they conclude it there. His **fifteen recommendations** are there, the only third-party proof in the entire estate. And his **entry point has always been the CEO or business owner**, which is a network motion, and the network is on LinkedIn. Every deal to date came out of that network; no channel outside it is known to have produced a single Blueprint enquiry.

Be honest about the gap. We have never seen him post.

- The live profile was bot-blocked on 3 September (HTTP 999). The headline, the About text and the recommendations could not be verified. Everything in the record comes from a July PDF export, and LinkedIn exports omit recommendations entirely.
- There is no posting history in the corpus: no frequency, no format, no reach, nothing. Any number in this kit about audience size would be invented, so there is none.
- His personal profile already runs Sales Navigator outreach automation for Lex Index, a venture explicitly out of scope. Whether Blueprint content and that automation can share one profile is unresolved, and it is a real constraint on everything below.

## The weekly rhythm

Xavier consults full time and builds his own infrastructure. The cadence below is sized so that a busy fortnight does not break it. **Two posts a week, and thirty minutes of replies.** That is the whole commitment.

| When | What | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every second Monday | Batch the next four posts from one real conversation each | 60 to 90 minutes |
| Tuesday and Thursday | Posts go out from the batch | Nothing, if batched |
| Across the week | Xavier replies to comments himself | 30 minutes, split how he likes |
| Monthly | Resurface one article that already exists. Write nothing new for it | 15 minutes |

The thirty minutes is the part that cannot be delegated and the part that produces conversations. A founder answering in the thread turns a reader into a conversation; an agency answering ends it. If that half hour is not available, the test is not being run, and we should say so rather than report it as a failure later.

## Content mix

The 70/30 rule from the 4 September call governs every asset: roughly 70% the SME's current problems, 30% history and proof. It lives **inside each post**, not across the month. The mix below only decides which problem leads.

Across a month of eight posts:

| Share | Post type | What it opens with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~35% | **The buyer's own sentence.** Something an owner actually said, and what it cost. | "We tried things with AI. Nothing worked." |
| ~25% | **Where AI does not pay off.** One idea that looks obvious and loses money. | The honest-sequencing line, in his flat register. |
| ~10% | **How to judge before spending.** The sizing arithmetic, two or three hours against twenty to thirty days. | A number, never an adjective. |
| ~20% | **Past to present.** One large-group programme, hinged into now. | The work, then the hinge. Never a CV. |
| ~10% | **Resurface.** One of the three published articles, or a speaking artefact. | The argument, not the announcement. |

The post skeleton, ready to fill. No call to action, no link on most posts.

## Organic is a test, not the strategy

This is the operating principle, and it is Xavier's framing. Organic is not dead. It is brutally competitive. So it is used as a free test to find what works before anything is leveraged, rather than as a channel we expect to carry the business on its own.

What that means in practice:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish to learn who answers">
    **Who responds matters, not how many.** Fifty consultants engaging is a failed test. Five SME owners is a passed one. Read every number as *of the right people*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the gates down in advance">
    Thresholds are set before the posts run, so the result cannot be graded generously afterwards. The August thresholds were written against a law-firm audience and need restating against the SME owner before the next block starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spend only on what already worked">
    Paid launches on Xavier's date, using copy that has already proven itself organically. Buying traffic first means paying to discover something a post tells us for free.
  </Step>
</Steps>

We commit to the cadence, the gates and the reporting. We do not promise lead volume from an organic test. Six weeks on one channel is a signal, not a forecast.
